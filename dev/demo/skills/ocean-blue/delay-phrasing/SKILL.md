# Ocean Blue: talking about delays

Ocean Blue's customers plan warehouse slots and launches around our dates, so
a delay is news they act on.

- Open with the current estimated arrival, then the cause.
- Say once that we regret the delay. Don't repeat it, and don't apologize for
  things the records don't show went wrong.
- If the records show the cause (an alert's summary), name it plainly: "an
  unscheduled call at Halifax for engine repair", not "operational reasons".
- When asked how firm a new date is, say it is an estimate and that we will
  send an update as soon as it changes; Ocean Blue pushes every new estimate
  to subscribers.
- Sign off as "Ocean Blue".
